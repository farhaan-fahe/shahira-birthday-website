CHAPTER 01

A little birthday wish ♡

✿✦✿

Happiest birthday Shahira, may Allah bless you with all the happiness, peace, and goodness your heart wishes for. May Allah accept all your duas, protect you from every harm and hardship, and guide you towards everything that is meant for you. May He bless you with good health, success, barakah, and a beautiful life ahead, and may all the dreams you carry in your heart come true in the best way and at the right time.

I hope this birthday brings you a new chapter filled with beautiful moments, genuine smiles, and countless reasons to be grateful. You have been a really special part of my life, and even though things between us are different now, that doesn’t change the fact that I sincerely wish the best for you.

I was genuinely looking forward to your birthday when things were okay between us, and I never imagined that by the time this day came, things would be so different. Still, today isn’t about the distance or the past; it’s simply about wishing you well.

I’m truly sorry for everything, for the things I did wrong, the things I regret, and for any way I may have hurt you. I carry that regret with me, but I’m not saying this expecting anything from you. I just wanted to be honest and wish you from my heart.

May Allah keep you surrounded by people who value you, keep your heart at peace, and protect you wherever life takes you. May He bless you not only today, but throughout every chapter of your life.

Happiest birthday once again, Shahira. May Allah always keep you happy and safe. 🫠🎀
